# Fonts

Subset copies built by `scripts/remote-fonts.sh`, of the same two faces the website uses. The
licensing is as `web/public/fonts/LICENSES.md` sets out: both are SIL Open Font License 1.1 with no
Reserved Font Name, so a subset may keep its name, and the notice and license travel with the
files, hence this file and the two `OFL-*.txt` beside it.

SIL Open Font License 1.1 · full text in `OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt`.

Patched with [Nerd Fonts](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts) 3.5.1, whose patcher is MIT
licensed and whose added glyph sets carry their own licenses. The private-use codepoints this
subset keeps come from Material Design Icons (Apache-2.0), Font Awesome (OFL-1.1), Powerline
Extra (MIT) and [Codicons](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode-codicons) (© Microsoft Corporation,
CC BY 4.0).

Subset to Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, punctuation, arrows, maths and box drawing, plus every glyph Luxe
draws. Regular (400), Bold (700) and Italic.
